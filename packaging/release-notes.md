Voges Slides: simple presentations for macOS, Windows and Linux. Part of the Voges suite, next to Voges Word, Voges Pencil and Voges Tables.

## What's new in Voges Slides 1.0.1

- **Linux:** the window shows the app's icon in Alt+Tab and in window lists.

## What Voges Slides does

- **Slides:** four layouts (title slide, title and content, title only, blank); add, duplicate, delete and reorder slides in the panel on the left, and give each one, or all of them, a background color.
- **Text:** font, size, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and color; alignment; bullets and numbering with levels; text at the top, middle or bottom of its shape.
- **Shapes and pictures:** rectangles, ellipses, triangles, stars, block arrows, lines and arrows with fill and outline, and PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP and WebP pictures.
- **Arranging:** move, resize, rotate, nudge with the arrows, select several, bring forward and send backward, duplicate, and copy, cut and paste between slides.
- **Slideshow:** F5 presents from the first slide and Shift+F5 from the current one, full screen.
- **Files:** opens `.pptx`, `.ppsx` and `.potx`, with the look the slides inherit from their theme, master and layout, and saves `.pptx`. Tables, charts, speaker notes, animations and transitions are not supported yet: they are left out when a file is opened, the app says so and saves to a new file, so the original stays as it is.
- Undo and redo for every change, and a warning before closing with unsaved changes.
- Updates itself through Voges Updater, which verifies every download against the signed `SHA256SUMS.txt` of the release.
- Interface in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

## Install

Page with the downloads and step-by-step instructions: https://omanoloneto.pro/voges/slides/

- **macOS** (10.15 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel): open `VogesSlides-macos.dmg` and drag Voges Slides to Applications. The first time, macOS blocks the app because it is not notarized by Apple: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
- **Windows** (10 or 11, 64-bit): run `VogesSlides-windows-setup.exe`; it does not need administrator rights. If SmartScreen warns you, click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
- **Linux** (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer and derivatives, 64-bit): `sudo apt install ./voges-slides-linux-amd64.deb`

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of each file, so you can verify the download, and `SHA256SUMS.txt.sig` is its signature.
